eShade Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This policy explains what the eShade app ("the app") does with information on your phone. The app is published by Dawn Tech Ltd. (דון טק בע"מ) ("we"). Contact: support@dawntech.co.il.

What the app uses and why

Bluetooth. The app connects to your eShade sunshade over Bluetooth Low Energy to open, close and configure it and to update its firmware. The device's status (position, battery, temperature, faults) is read over this connection and shown in the app.

Location. The app uses your phone's location for two things:

  • to show the local weather, by sending your approximate coordinates to the Open-Meteo weather service (open-meteo.com), which returns the temperature and conditions;
  • to check whether the car is moving, so the shade cannot be opened while driving. Your speed is computed on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

You can deny location access. Weather will then not be shown and the driving check will be off.

Information you enter. Your name and contact details in Profile, the names you give your eShade devices, vehicle details (make, model, year) and device PIN codes are stored only on your phone.

What we do not do

  • The app has no account and does not send your data to our servers.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or "Forget all devices" and clearing your profile in the app, deletes it. "Factory reset" in the app also clears the settings stored on the eShade device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's use of information changes and change the date above.
