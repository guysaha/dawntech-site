The problem

A parked car turns into an oven.

In one hour in the summer sun, the inside of a car gets far hotter than the air outside. Dashboards fade and crack, electronics suffer, and the wheel and seats become painful to touch.

Surface and air temperatures after 1 hour parked in the sun on a ~38 °C day. Source: Arizona State University & UC San Diego, Temperature, 2018.

And the usual fix hasn't changed in decades.

The folding shade

  • Unfold, fit, remove, fold and store it. Every single time.
  • Ends up on the back seat instead of on the glass.
  • Sags, falls and wears out.
  • Meanwhile the dashboard fades and cracks, and the wheel and seat-belt buckle get too hot to touch.

eShade

  • Install once. It stays on the windshield, ready.
  • Shade or unshade with one tap, from the unit or your phone.
  • Reflective heat and UV barrier across the glass.
  • Retracts into a slim side housing, nothing to store.

How it works

Three steps. The first one you only do once.

  1. 1

    Install once

    Attach eShade to the inside of your windshield with its silicone suction cups, adjust the three-axis mount, and set how far the shade should travel. That's it.

  2. 2

    Tap Shade when you park

    Press the button on the unit or tap Shade in the app. The shade glides across the glass and stops at the coverage you chose.

  3. 3

    Tap Unshade and drive

    The shade rolls back into its housing along the edge of the windshield. The app won't let it deploy while the car is moving.

The invention

The Spiral Piston.

At the heart of eShade is our patented Spiral Piston. A metal band lies flat and coiled inside the compact housing. As a motorized drum turns, it unrolls into a straight, rigid arm that carries the shade across the windshield, and pulls it back again.

Long reach, tiny footprint. That's what lets a full windshield shade live in a unit the size of your hand.

FAQ

Questions and answers.

What exactly is eShade?

An electric sunshade for your car's windshield. It mounts on the inside of the windshield and moves a reflective shade across the glass at the touch of a button or from the app. When you're ready to drive, it retracts into a slim housing along the edge of the windshield.

How do I control it?

Two ways: the eShade app on your phone, over Bluetooth, or the two buttons on the unit itself. A short press moves the shade all the way; holding a button moves it only while you hold. In the app you can also stop anywhere or slide to the exact coverage you want.

How is it powered? Does it use my car battery?

No wiring to the car at all. eShade has its own rechargeable battery, kept charged by a built-in solar panel in everyday use. If you use it very often or park in the shade for long periods, you can top it up over USB.

What happens when the battery runs low?

The app shows the battery level at all times. If there isn't enough charge to finish covering the windshield, eShade won't start shading, so it never gets stuck halfway across the glass. Unshading is still allowed, so you can always clear your view.

Can the shade open while I'm driving?

No. The app uses your phone's location to tell when the car is moving and won't deploy the shade while you drive. When retracted, the shade sits in its housing at the edge of the windshield, out of your line of sight.

What if something is in the way?

eShade senses when its movement is blocked and stops by itself. The app tells you what happened so you can clear the way and try again.

Will it fit my car?

eShade is designed to fit most passenger cars. The mount adjusts on three axes, attaches with silicone suction cups, and you set the travel distance once for your windshield. Not sure about your model? Email us and we'll check.

Do I need a technician to install it?

No. Installation is a one-time job you do yourself: position the unit, adjust the mount, and set the travel distance in the app. After that it simply stays in place.

Can someone else control my eShade?

Not if you set a PIN. Each eShade can be protected with a 4-digit code, and only phones that know it can operate the shade. Control works over Bluetooth, so you need to be near the car anyway.

Can I control more than one car?

Yes. The app remembers all your eShades, each with its own name, and connects to the one you choose.

Where do I get the app? Which phones are supported?

Android phones running 7.0 or newer can download the app here; when Android asks, allow your browser to install it. It will also come to Google Play. An iPhone app is coming soon.

How does eShade get updates?

New firmware comes with app updates. When your eShade runs an older version, the app offers to update it over Bluetooth in a couple of minutes.

Is the technology patented?

Yes. The Spiral Piston mechanism is protected by granted patents in the United States (US 12,157,351 B2) and China (CN 114929075 B), with applications pending in Europe and Australia. See the patents.

Can the shade carry my company's logo?

Yes. The shade can be printed with custom designs for fleets, dealerships, sports teams and other brands. Contact us to discuss your project.

eShade is getting ready for launch. Email us and we'll let you know as soon as it's available. Distributors and fleet operators are welcome to reach out now.
